Late Morning at the Delphoria

By half past nine the lobby had lost what little patience it started with. A desk clerk tore yesterday's date off a pad, somebody in the cocktail lounge dragged a chair across the floor upstairs, and the old phone behind the Sports Desk rang often enough that nobody bothered pretending it was a quiet hotel.

The eight-o'clock sheet was still under the glass. San Francisco had been +180. Toronto had been +154. A runner came in with the final morning paper and slid it across the scarred counter without asking for Jesse. Nobody asked for Jesse. They just waited until he appeared.

San Francisco was now +182, but the fair number had gone from +182 to +184. Jesse read both columns once. Toronto was worse: the useful +154 had disappeared and the only fresh number left was +147. He tapped that slip with one finger and moved it aside.

Then the big numbers showed up. Beşiktaş draw, +950 DraftKings and +750 Bet365. Vasalunds draw, +650 Bet365 and +500 DraftKings. The numbers looked like action until the gaps between the books were written beside them. Two hundred cents on one. A hundred-fifty on the other. Jesse did not need the desk to explain that something could look big and still look wrong.

Lech–Thun had gone the other direction. The +425 DraftKings number from eight was gone; +375 Bet365 was what remained. Atlanta was still −109. St. Louis still −108 at the only fresh shop that mattered.

JESSE: “Seven slips. Big numbers, bad numbers, old numbers. Same answer. Nobody's got action.”
