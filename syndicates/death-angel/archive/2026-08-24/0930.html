The Evening at the Delphoria Starts Before Noon

The cocktail lounge was still half dark when the morning sheet came through. The bartender had not opened the register yet. A stack of clean glasses sat upside down behind the bar, the house music was off, and a man from maintenance was trying to make the red EXIT sign stop buzzing without unplugging the thing that made it an exit sign.

The Sports Desk clerk laid the 09:16 sheet flat on the scarred counter and made two piles.

Four slips went under the red PASS stamp. Three stayed loose.

The first loose one carried a number large enough to make a lounge regular stop pretending not to listen: Roma–Fiorentina first-half over 3.5, Bet365 +1800.

The clerk read the other book: +1340.

The difference was 460 cents.

A runner took both numbers through the lounge. Jesse was at a side table with an untouched glass in front of him, watching the room rather than the television.

RUNNER: “Eighteen hundred. Other place says thirteen-forty.”
JESSE: “Same slip?”
RUNNER: “Same half. Same total.”
JESSE: “Then they don't agree.”

The runner waited.

JESSE: “Leave it open. Don't touch it.”

The second loose slip was Cecilia Zandalasini over 2.5 rebounds. DraftKings showed +132. Bet365 showed −116. Same player, same prop, same line, two very different answers. Her participation and role were not confirmed.

JESSE: “No player, no bet. No agreement, no bet. Put it by the phone.”

The third loose slip had been around earlier: Griffin Conine over 0.5 runs scored. Bet365 still showed +240, DraftKings +176. The starting lineup was still missing.

JESSE: “Still waiting on the lineup?”
RUNNER: “Yeah.”
JESSE: “Then nothing changed.”

One old number had changed enough to matter. Esteury Ruiz had been a WAIT at +190 on the 08:00 sheet. The current DraftKings price was +159, Bet365 +132. The clerk crossed out the old +190 with a grease pencil before the runner reached the lounge.

Jesse looked at the crossed-out line once.

JESSE: “That's gone. File it.”

Osasuna–Levante under 2.5 stayed too expensive. Roma–Fiorentina over 2.5 had improved but still did not clear the price gate. Neom SC showed +550 at one shop and +425 at the other with kickoff close enough that nobody upstairs wanted a speech about it.

The bartender turned one of the clean glasses upright.

The Sports Desk drawer stayed shut.
