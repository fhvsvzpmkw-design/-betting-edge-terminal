From Around the Delphoria

The Delphoria Hotel is old enough that the room keys are still real keys and the switchboard still knows who called whom. The Sports Desk started as a place for guests to get scores and racing results. Jesse gradually added lines, then a second telephone, then a locked drawer. Nobody remembers an opening ceremony.

By daylight it passes for a hotel service. After dinner the same desk gets quieter, the calls get shorter, and people stop asking for scores they could have found themselves.

BAINS: “Front desk gives you a room. Sports Desk tells you whether the number's worth staying up for.”
