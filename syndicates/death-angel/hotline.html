The Morning at the Delphoria

The first sheet came off the desk a little after six. Cheap coffee on the counter. Old smoke still in the lounge. Somebody had left a carbon phone slip under the register with GIANTS +189 written hard enough to mark the copy underneath.

That one stayed alive. Betting Edge called it a LEAN: +185 or better was the number, but the books were too far apart, the Giants were sending Matt Wilkinson out for his major-league debut, and the lineups were still hanging. Jesse looked at the slip, told the clerk to keep the phone open, and went back toward the private rooms.

By eight the same file came back with a prettier number and a worse problem. DraftKings showed +195—but the time stamp was 06:42. The fresh Bet365 screen said +161. Jesse read both, folded the old slip once, and pushed it away.

JESSE: “Old paper. New number's no good. Keep the phone open.”
