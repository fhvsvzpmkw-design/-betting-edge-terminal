No Ticket. Same Dirty Counter.

By dusk the old card is scratched out, the phones are still ringing, and Jesse is waiting for somebody to bring him a number that does not smell wrong.

At 15:15 five afternoon names were still sitting on the counter. White Sox +158. Yankees −109. Miami +190. Milwaukee −172. Sparks −135. Five passes. The clerk kept the sheet under a chipped ashtray until the games started taking themselves off the board.

The Delphoria does not get quieter so much as change shifts. Lounge noise leaks down the hall. A phone rings beside a stack of carbon slips. Somebody crosses out another game with a grease pencil. The elevator complains, opens, and closes again.

Jesse is not making a line. Somebody brings him the Betting Edge sheet; he decides whether he wants his money anywhere near it. Nothing from the afternoon survives.

At 18:08 a runner drops four new names on the counter: Sultanes, Tacoma, Charros and Herediano. Four more PASS marks. Jesse looks at Sultanes twice and the rest once.
