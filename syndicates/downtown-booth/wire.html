Everybody Finally Found the Dodgers

DOWNTOWN — The Dodgers sat around -250 through the morning and nobody made a speech about it. By the afternoon the screen showed -270 at one shop and -278 elsewhere. That's when everybody suddenly had a reason.

Vic's reaction from the back booth: “Maybe they're right. Doesn't mean I wanna be the last one through the door.”

15:16 // LITTLE PIECE OF PAPER

Padres +115 or Nothing

Vic still has San Diego written on the small sheet beside the coffee cup. +105 appeared. Then +110. The number he wants remains +115.

“Sometimes the bookmaker has to come to you. If he doesn't, you finish your coffee and go home.”

15:18 // SOMEBODY ALREADY ATE

Cubs -161: The Check Is on the Table

“Good team. Nice matchup. Everybody nodding. My number was -138. At -161 somebody already had dinner and left you the check.”

Vic says the dangerous part of a favorite isn't being wrong about the team. It's being so pleased with the team that you stop noticing what you're paying.

15:20 // BOOTH STATUS

A Quiet Board Is Still a Board

No ticket was written. Vic appears delighted by this.

“Casino's open tomorrow. The guys who need a ticket every day eventually start calling the ATM a partner.”
