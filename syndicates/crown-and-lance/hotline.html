The Doors Open

Tonight's star: Alex Daventry. By mid-afternoon the Crown & Lance is not open yet, but it is already loud. The kitchen is testing the ovens, two knights are arguing over which horse belongs on which side of the arena, and somebody has hung the blue banner upside down.

Alex has been on the floor since lunch. Years ago, as Gwydion, he learned that rushing a dangerous task before every ingredient was in place tended to end badly. A medieval dinner show is less dangerous than Manannan's house, but not by enough to ignore the lesson.

Before the First Trumpet

The first rehearsal charge ends when the red knight's shield strap breaks and skids halfway across the arena. The blue gallery, although empty, receives a full bow from the actor playing the herald.

Alex walks out, picks up the shield and hands it back without comment.

From the kitchen door comes a different problem: the chicken count is right, the potatoes are late and one crate of goblets has been delivered to the wedding chapel next door. The banquet captain asks whether to open the wine early.

“No. We already know where it is. That's not the same thing as needing it now.”

The captain takes the answer, because this is the tone Alex uses when he has already spent most of his youth waiting for the correct moment to do something considerably stranger.

By 15:20 the shield is fixed, the potatoes have arrived and a page comes across the arena with the folded VigScope sheet tucked under one arm.

Between Bouts

Alex takes the sheet into the narrow service corridor behind the royal box. No audience. No trumpets. No herald. Just the afternoon board.

Six names are already crossed out PASS.

One remains open: Shakira Austin under 2.5 assists. The best number has improved from +121 in the morning to +125, and the two approved books are much closer than they were earlier. But the issued instruction still says WAIT for +140 or better after active-status confirmation.

Alex reads it once, folds the sheet, and puts it back inside his coat.

“Better is useful. Ready is different.”

That is the entire betting conversation. The rest of the afternoon belongs to dinner.
