MoneyPuck edge is its published full-game win forecast minus the recorded moneyline’s break-even probability, in percentage points. We show the best captured price for each side and rank one side per game.

Pinnacle edge is the no-vig market comparison. MoneyPuck’s published forecast can include market input; its model component appears separately in the game details.

A positive gap is a forecast comparison. The last issued VigScope BET, LEAN, WAIT or PASS remains its own call at its recorded price.

Hockey forecasts: MoneyPuck.com. Published full-game pregame probabilities, with OT included. Each game keeps its source link and capture time.
