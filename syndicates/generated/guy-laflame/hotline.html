How Guy’s record is counted

We retain the highest positive MoneyPuck edge actually captured during the day, using eligible NHL full-game moneylines and quotes no more than 75 minutes old when recorded. The daily leader freezes one minute before its own game starts. Until then, a higher edge on another upcoming game can take the lead and move the cutoff to that game’s start. An early game elsewhere on the slate does not close the window. Once frozen, later opportunities cannot replace that day’s pick. Exact ties retain the earlier recorded candidate. Earlier locked or skipped days keep their original cutoff.

Edge = MoneyPuck’s published full-game win probability minus the price’s break-even probability, in percentage points. MoneyPuck can include market input. This is separate hypothetical value tracking; it does not change an issued VigScope call or confirm a customer wager.

Final scores include overtime and shootouts. A win returns the saved odds’ profit on 1u risk; a loss is −1u. ROI uses settled picks only. Pending, skipped days and unresolved results contribute no profit or loss. Tracking began Oct 6, 5:50 PM PT; earlier games are not backfilled.
