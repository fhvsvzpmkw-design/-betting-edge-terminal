# Qualified market price test v1

This is a prospective hypothetical test of market-based price selection, starting **September 16, 2026 at 00:00 Pacific**. It grants no betting authority and does not change Core thresholds, stakes, card decisions or past issued reports. The fixed rule and activation are in `market-method-shadow-v1.json`; each snapshot records their SHA-256 fingerprint. Changing that rule requires a new version and a future activation date.

For each available full-game moneyline, spread/run/puck line or total, compare the exact obtainable Bet365 or DraftKings quote with its qualified exact paired Pinnacle no-vig reference. Review the two opposing sides together. Choose the strongest strictly positive probability-point discrepancy, using the best qualifying supported-book price and deterministic identity/book tie-breaks. Integer and half-point lines are supported; quarter lines are excluded. There is no winning-outcome selection, top-N cutoff or retrospective threshold tuning.

The entry must have a current source-linked information review, resolved material conflicts, recorded compatible settlement semantics, exact identity and scope, and fresh bound quote observations. An independently calibrated forecast is not an entry condition in this separate test. Missing forecast adoption can leave a card draft unissued while its completed current-information evidence supports this hypothetical test; such origin is explicitly labelled. Missing current-information work excludes the candidate and remains visible with a reason. All examined quotes, including unfavorable prices and qualifying quotes that lost the deterministic comparison, remain accounted for in the exclusion records.

A modeled BET or LEAN can supply the existing receipt's `candidateAssessment.personnel` review: `RESOLVED` or `NOT_MATERIAL`, a rationale, official/reporting source IDs, and the current bound review time. Where underlying personnel remains partial or unresolved, the review must retain both a materiality explanation and the remaining uncertainty; a label alone cannot erase a discrepancy. For those modeled cards, settlement evidence comes from their adopted exact `fairValueEvidence.forecastRecordIds`, raw forecast `settlement` fields, and current `applicability` or sidecar `revalidations` with `settlementMatch: true` and a rationale. Both unconditional and conditional forecast probability bases are permitted as settlement evidence; the experimental Pinnacle probability remains explicitly conditional on no push. No new market-only assessment object is required for an otherwise valid modeled card.

The report publisher freezes `report.marketMethodShadow` and the corresponding sidecar field at publication. The snapshot includes bound feed/observer hashes, original quote change times, current observation times, evidence sources and both sides of the qualified Pinnacle pair. Reobserving an unchanged quote does not reset its movement timestamp. This is evidence of an obtainable observed price, not proof of an actual fill.

The evaluator counts the **first qualifying published appearance for each exact market per Pacific day**. Later reports cannot replace its side or entry price, and cannot add that opposing side as another sample. Line changes are distinct exact contracts. Reports on different days can count the same future event again; unique events and the correlation limitation are reported, so sample counts are never presented as independent statistical trials. Historical replay is development evidence only and is excluded from prospective performance.

Returns use one hypothetical unit at the frozen entry decimal price. A verified win earns `decimal - 1`, a loss earns `-1`, and a push or void earns `0`. Unverified or missing exact settlements remain unresolved. ROI divides net units by settled hypothetical entries including pushes and voids; unresolved entries stay out of that denominator and remain counted separately. Conditional no-push reference probability is never presented as unconditional expected ROI.

An exact same-book verified observation is the preferred settlement evidence. An unissued candidate, or an entry at a different book or line, can also be graded from existing verified final scores when the source report confirms the same event, start time, sport, full-game scope **and market family** (moneyline, spread/run/puck line, or total). A settled moneyline alone cannot establish run-line or total settlement; shortened-game rules can differ. The evaluator applies the candidate's frozen side and line to a compatible score and preserves every source run and verification reference. Supported score replay sports are MLB, NBA/WNBA, NFL, NCAAF, CFL and NHL. Conflicting scores, unsupported scopes and moneyline ties without explicit tie settlement stay unresolved. This adds no score requests, schedules or observation-file changes.

Price diagnostics require an actually observed fresh exact same-book price after entry and before event start. The existing observer supplies a **last observed pre-start price**, which is not asserted to be a closing line. Missing comparisons remain missing. A verified closing label requires explicit verification in both the observation and its method. The price ratio is not a de-vigged closing edge or evidence of true profitability.

`build-results-index.mjs` can embed the evaluation in its existing derived results without adding a schedule. The standalone read-only evaluator is:

```sh
node tools/market-method-shadow.mjs evaluate --root .
```

An optional `--output <derived-file.json>` writes only the evaluation artifact. The evaluator never rebuilds or rewrites historical snapshots, card grades or observation files. The test has no automatic path to betting authority.
