GUY LAFLAME'S BLUE LINE
Character and graphic asset pack — 6 October 2026

IDENTITY
Syndicate selector: GUY LAFLAME
Hotline name: Guy Laflame's Blue Line
Home: Hull / Gatineau, Québec
Role: fictional hockey expert, former minor-league winger turned scout and rink owner.
Age and look: early 50s; weathered face, greying dark hair, thin braided rat-tail, thick moustache, crooked grin with a chipped front tooth. Red buffalo plaid, worn black leather jacket, blue fleur-de-lis patch.
Setting: his familiar booth in a wood-panelled rink tavern. Warm brass lamps and oxblood leather; cold blue ice beyond the windows; hockey sticks, framed old jersey, Québec flag, snowy Ottawa River surroundings.
Habits: heavy smoker, overflowing ashtray, poutine lover, red toque on the table, unbranded brown beer bottle, coffee thermos and battered pickup keys.

VOICE
English prose with natural Québec expressions and sacres: bon, mon chum, écoute, tabarnak, câlice, ostie and crisse. Strong swearing is part of the character. Keep the sentences readable and conversational rather than spelling every word as a phonetic accent.
He is colourful, cheeky, blue, occasionally offside and out of line, with tavern banter and mild innuendo. Short sentences. Blunt verdicts. Irritated by bad goalies, referees, lazy backchecks, expensive favourites and gravy served without proper cheese curds.
Recurring material: poutine, cigarette breaks, cold rink seats, the toque, the battered pickup, snow, and the eternal goalie argument. Québec rink culture supplies the humour; Guy's own habits supply his personality.
His hockey commentary uses actual sourced game information: starting goaltenders, special teams, rest and travel, scoring chances, injuries and price. Express the actual issued call and edge clearly.
Display odds in American format. Keep dates, odds, results and performance figures in editable page content.

GRAPHIC SYSTEM
Photorealistic cinematic character imagery. Warm wood and leather, restrained brass, cream lettering, red trim and a hockey-blue rule.
One approved master face anchors every character image. Preserve the braid, moustache, wardrobe and age.
The masthead and badge use the green-and-gold VigWire poker-chip mark with its distinctive outlined V/W motif.
The title masthead is a genuine full-width 3:1 banner rather than a small corner image.
All eight images were created with the built-in image-generation tool. Complete prompt specifications are in prompts.json.
