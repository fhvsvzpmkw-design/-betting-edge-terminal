Effective September 22, 2026, for the user-requested personnel/QB input review. This integrates the user's accepted primary/weighted/equal-share Graham replacement convention in the existing personnel staging writer. It does not change the frozen registry, QB model, carried ratings, historical receipts or betting authority.

Submit `RESOLVED_MODEL_ESTIMATE` only for a source-supported current non-QB absence with calibrated healthy and replacement values. Use model `graham-replacement-role-estimate-v1` and retain `GRAHAM_MODEL_ESTIMATE` provenance. The production contract explicitly permits PRIMARY_REPLACEMENT, WEIGHTED_COMMITTEE and EQUAL_SHARE_COMMITTEE. Unequal candidate values alone no longer block the approved committee assumption. These are Graham estimates, not a formula attributed to Billy Walters or verified workload.

Bind availabilitySourceRefs, roleSourceRefs and every model source to the case's sourceRefs. Supply a current active replacement identity, role rationale and explicit reopenOn trigger. A reported absence pending the formal team designation must retain that qualification. A depth-chart reserve alone does not prove a measured share or confirmed starter.

Require estimateAcknowledged, assumptionRationale, baselineTreatment=ADDITIONAL_DUTIES_ONLY, baselineDutiesDisplaced=false, baselineRationale and baselineSourceIds. Preserve each reserve's existing duties and estimate only additional vacant-role duties. Exclude baseline starters from replacement credit unless their duties are separately reconciled; displaced roles are outside this adapter. No unavailable player or duplicate replacement across simultaneous cases is allowed. Existing cluster guards remain in force.

Loss is max(0, frozen healthy value minus estimated replacement value), rounded once to 0.001 points. Preserve the method, weights, numerator/denominator, excluded upgrade and candidate-endpoint sensitivity. Equal shares are an assumption; the range is allocation sensitivity, not a confidence interval or exhaustive uncertainty bound. A calibrated zero loss does not mean the absence has no football impact.

The existing personnel workflow owns validation, arithmetic, additive events, fair overlays and durable ledger writes. Existing QB reconciliation and terminal refresh follow. Games containing estimates retain personnelEstimateCases; otherwise complete games use READY_WITH_PERSONNEL_MODEL_ESTIMATES. Unresolved personnel or QB inputs still take precedence. Missing values, uncertain replacement availability, QB/impairment estimates, displaced duties and unreviewed OL/DB/LB/RB clusters remain blocked. This adapter does not extend M4 or nonzero matchup permissions.

This targeted manual review is not a scheduled 32-team sweep and does not close or advance a scheduled research runtime.
