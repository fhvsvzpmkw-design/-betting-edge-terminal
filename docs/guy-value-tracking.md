# Guy’s daily MoneyPuck value record

`data/characters/guy-laflame/value-ledger.json` holds the prospective ledger. `tools/track-guy-value.mjs` updates it after every successful shared game-intelligence collection and during the existing 03:00 Pacific recovery. The public record is shown on Guy’s Hotline and in VigScope Value, below the Graham comparison. Both views fetch saved data without caching on open/reload; Guy’s Refresh Rundown also reloads the record.

The daily leader remains open until one minute before **its own scheduled NHL start**, regardless of earlier games elsewhere on the slate. Each successful collection can replace it with a strictly higher positive MoneyPuck full-game moneyline edge on another upcoming game, moving the cutoff to that game’s start. Quotes must be observed within 75 minutes of recording; Pinnacle is a benchmark, not an offered tracking book. Exact ties retain the earlier candidate. At the leader’s cutoff the saved selection freezes even if the next workflow runs later. Subsequent edges cannot replace it, so there remains at most one locked pick per Pacific day. If the highest captured leader is an early game, it still freezes early.

With no positive leader yet, collection continues until the final game’s one-minute cutoff or the Pacific day ends. Games whose own cutoff has passed cannot become new leaders, even if their saved pregame price or forecast looks better. Captures are recorded at the actual workflow clock; earlier source timestamps do not backdate selection. Installation after an early game can still track a later upcoming game, but cannot reconstruct an already-started selection. A price captured earlier in the window is a hypothetical tracking price; collection is not continuous and the price is not promised to remain available at the cutoff.

The policy is `daily-peak-before-selected-puck-v2`. Migration retains all original picks, odds, results and observation receipts. Already locked or skipped entries, including a legacy WATCHING entry whose original cutoff has passed, retain their original cutoff and do not reopen. An unclosed legacy leader moves to its own game’s cutoff; policy IDs and the policy-change timestamp identify the transition.

Each observation retains the team, event and scheduled start, bookmaker, decimal price for exact calculations, displayed American odds, forecast, break-even probability, edge in percentage points, model/quote observation times and actual ledger recording time. The original observation receipts remain in the daily entry when the leader changes. The recorded odds are never repriced for settlement. Earlier source captures are not imported as Guy picks; installation after the final eligible window records a skipped day.

Grading reuses the shared source grader’s exact-event verified final results. NHL full-game moneylines include overtime/shootouts. A win returns `priceDecimal - 1` units on one unit risk; a loss is `-1`. Net/ROI exclude pending and unresolved entries. With no settled picks, ROI is null and shown as a dash. Special or tied finals require settlement review. Scores and source links remain attached to the record. This is hypothetical value tracking, distinct from issued BET/LEAN/WAIT/PASS calls and customer wagers. MoneyPuck’s published probability can include market input.

Run `node --test tests/guy-value.test.mjs tests/guy-blue-line.test.mjs` to verify capture, cutoffs, no backfill, quote freshness, exact identities, outcomes, returns and display. `node tools/track-guy-value.mjs` advances the saved ledger at the current real clock. `node tools/build-guy-hotline.mjs --preview` refreshes only the static fallback; it does not rewrite published archives.
