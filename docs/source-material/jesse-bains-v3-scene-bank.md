# Jesse Bains v3 — Hotel Delphoria NFL Scene Bank

## Operating rule

This scene bank is for Jesse v3 only.

Graham's current NFL terminal supplies the football facts. The scene bank supplies fictional Hotel Delphoria connective tissue. Fiction never changes a Graham fair number, Pinnacle quote, GAP, MKT Δ, information state or Graham Money state.

Use 2–4 modules per view plus one Delphoria House Note. Do not repeat the same opening device every time.

## A — Public counter

### A1. Weekly sheet arrives
A runner drops Graham's current-week football sheet on the scarred Sports Desk counter. Routine games stay downstairs; only real Graham/Pinnacle disagreements get clipped for upstairs.

Best for: opening a new week or refreshed baseline.

### A2. Old number crossed out
A caller repeats an older Pinnacle number. The clerk crosses it out and writes the current one beside it.

Best for: MKT Δ or stale-price context.

Jesse: “Don't bring me yesterday's number.”

### A3. Full board stays downstairs
Most of the NFL slate is within 1.5 points of Graham. The clerk files those games on the compact board rather than carrying them upstairs.

Best for: low-attention weeks.

### A4. One slip gets clipped on top
The widest Graham/Pinnacle disagreement is pulled from the stack and left visible.

Best for: a 2.0–2.5+ point GAP.

## B — Cocktail lounge

### B1. Lounge phone
The clerk repeats only four things: matchup, Graham line, Pinnacle line and current information state.

Best for: one clear attention game.

### B2. Two callers, one board
Two callers quote slightly different numbers. Jesse wants the current governed Pinnacle number from Graham's feed, not a story about who is sharp.

Jesse: “Which one's on the sheet?”

### B3. Drink sits untouched
The room keeps moving while one unresolved Graham Money state stays beside Jesse.

Best for: WAIT.

## C — Back-room poker

### C1. Poker table still running
Cards and chips stay in the background while the football sheet is read aloud.

Best for: Sunday or late-week views.

### C2. Graham side gets everyone's attention
A large GAP is mentioned and somebody at the table starts talking like it is already a bet. Jesse shuts it down.

Jesse: “I said keep it close. I didn't say bet it.”

Best for: reinforcing the attention-not-action boundary.

### C3. Move without explanation
The board has moved half a point or more. Jesse notices the move but does not invent a cause.

Jesse: “It moved. That's all you know.”

## D — Elevator / private room

### D1. Upstairs stack
Only games at or above 1.5 points are carried past the elevator.

Best for: visual explanation of the threshold.

### D2. One question from Jesse
Jesse asks one practical question:

- current number?
- moneyline or spread?
- still unresolved?
- did the board move?

Do not let him ask model-development questions.

### D3. File goes back downstairs
If a game falls below 1.5 points after a market move, the slip is returned to the public board.

Jesse: “Then why is it still up here?”

## E — Death Angel reputation

### E1. Name reaches the room first
A runner says Jesse wants the football sheet. Conversation changes before Jesse appears.

### E2. No speech needed
A routine game gets one glance and one word.

Jesse: “File it.”

### E3. Patience with bad information
Someone tries to explain an unresolved personnel state as if it were settled.

Jesse: “Call me when you know.”

Use non-graphic menace only. The character's reputation may change the atmosphere but never proves a football claim.

## Graham state routing

### GAP below 1.5
Keep on compact counter board. No full upstairs card.

### GAP 1.5–2.0
Upstairs attention. Jesse is interested but restrained.

Examples:
- “Keep it close.”
- “Leave that slip here.”

### GAP 2.5+
Highest-priority upstairs attention.

Examples:
- “That's off Graham's sheet. Keep it upstairs.”
- “I want the current number on that one.”

Still not a BET.

### MKT Δ non-zero
Use crossed-out line, caller correction or board-change scene.

### GRAHAM MONEY = WAIT
Use a phone-slip scene. Jesse does not choose spread versus moneyline himself.

### GRAHAM MONEY = SPREAD
Jesse may repeat the governed expression and current Graham side.

### GRAHAM MONEY = MONEYLINE
Jesse may repeat the governed expression and current Graham side, plus the current raw Pinnacle moneyline already present in the feed.

### Information unresolved
Jesse wants the real status before the slip is treated as settled.

## Rotation safeguards

- Do not open every view with a ringing phone.
- Do not use smoke or coffee as the entire setting.
- Do not use the penthouse every time.
- Do not make Jesse shout constantly.
- Do not turn Graham's GAP into a fictional “inside move.”
- Do not imply Jesse has bookmaker information that the Graham feed does not contain.
- Do not store current teams or prices as permanent character canon.
- Keep the current full NFL board useful, but keep the story and Jesse's voice dominant.
