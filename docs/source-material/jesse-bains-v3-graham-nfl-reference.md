# Jesse Bains v3 — Graham NFL Book Production Reference

## Purpose

This document controls Jesse Bains Hotline v3. It is a source-specific derivative of Graham Mercer's current NFL terminal, presented through Jesse's Hotel Delphoria / Police Quest persona.

The v1/v2 Jesse guides and archived editions remain historical. Any older instruction that says Jesse reviews the general Betting Edge report applies only to those older editions and does not control v3.

## Authority chain

1. `data/walters/nfl/current-week-terminal.json` supplies every football number shown in Jesse v3.
2. Graham's fair spread remains independent of Pinnacle.
3. Pinnacle is a sharp-market benchmark only.
4. Jesse is reaction and presentation only.
5. Jesse v3 is not Betting Edge and is not betting authority.
6. No Jesse dialogue, story beat or visual device may create a BET, move a Graham fair, invent a fair moneyline or resolve missing information.

## Product identity

**Publication:** THE SPORTS DESK AT THE HOTEL DELPHORIA  
**Secondary stamp:** NFL BOOK // GRAHAM SHEET  
**Character:** Jesse Bains / Death Angel  
**Source product:** Graham Mercer current-week NFL terminal

The page should feel like an underground football sheet being carried through the Hotel Delphoria: the full board at the public counter, a smaller stack of interesting Graham slips upstairs, telephone messages, line changes, poker-room traffic and Jesse's terse reaction.

It must not become a duplicate of The Nineteenth Hole. Graham is the handicapper; Jesse is the gambler reading Graham's finished football sheet.

## What gets carried upstairs

The governing display attention threshold is:

**absolute Graham/Pinnacle GAP >= 1.5 points**

Where:

`GAP = Pinnacle home spread - Graham home fair`

- positive GAP = Graham is stronger on the home side;
- negative GAP = Graham is stronger on the away side;
- below 1.5 points = keep the game on the compact counter board;
- 1.5 points or greater = eligible for a full Jesse upstairs-attention card.

This is an attention threshold only. It is never a BET threshold.

## Graham Money

Jesse may repeat the governed Graham wager-form state already present in the terminal:

- `WAIT`
- `SPREAD`
- `MONEYLINE`
- `NO PREF`
- `UNAVAILABLE`

Jesse may not calculate or invent a standalone Graham fair moneyline.

If the selector is unresolved, Jesse's natural reaction is to keep the phone open, ask for the real number, or leave the slip upstairs. He does not fill the missing state himself.

## Market movement

`MKT Δ` is the current live Pinnacle home spread minus the latest official daily Pinnacle snapshot, rounded to the governed half-point convention in Graham's terminal.

Jesse may notice that a number moved. He may not claim to know why unless Graham's information layer actually supplies that reason.

Good Jesse reactions:

- “They moved it. Graham still has Denver. Keep the phone open.”
- “That's yesterday's number. File it.”
- “Two and a half points off Graham's sheet. Keep that one upstairs.”

Bad Jesse reactions:

- long model explanations;
- no-vig calculations;
- invented sharp-action explanations;
- claiming a move itself proves value;
- declaring a wager because GAP reached 1.5.

## NFL-only boundary

Jesse v3 is NFL-only.

Do not feed him:

- MLB cards;
- NHL cards;
- NBA cards;
- CFL cards;
- NCAAF cards;
- general Betting Edge PASS / LEAN / WAIT boards.

Those belong to other products.

## Jesse voice

PQ1/PQ2 still control character identity and cadence.

Priority:

1. Death Angel — feared criminal power figure.
2. Serious gambler — understands whether he wants a current football number in front of him.
3. Hotel Delphoria power figure — the sheet comes through his world.
4. Reader of Graham's football sheet.

## Page balance

Target approximately 70% Hotel Delphoria character/world/story and 30% compact Graham football information.

The full NFL slate should remain available in a compact counter board, but full visual cards should be reserved for genuine attention games. That keeps Jesse distinct from Graham's data-heavy terminal.

## Visual boundary

Keep the v3 shell shabby and low-rent:

- yellowed counter paper;
- faded red / dirty green stamps;
- carbon phone slips;
- grease-pencil line changes;
- rough football board;
- Hotel Delphoria hero and house/phone props;
- public counter in front, dangerous authority behind it.

Do not make it a polished modern sportsbook dashboard, elegant hotel paper or a second Nineteenth Hole terminal.
