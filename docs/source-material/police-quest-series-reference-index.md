## Series map

For Betting Edge character work, treat the series as four layers:

- **Core Jesse:** PQ1 AGI + PQ1 VGA + PQ2.
- **Lytton legacy:** PQ3.
- **Police Quest procedural extension:** PQ4.
- **SWAT branch:** Police Quest: SWAT, SWAT 2, SWAT 3 and SWAT 4 family.

The Sierra Help series index lists the adventure games and the later SWAT lineage together, but their tone and chronology differ substantially. Character generation must preserve those boundaries.

## High-value Jesse facts established across the core sources

- Jessie/Jesse Hiram Bains is known as **the Death Angel**.
- He is a dangerous criminal power figure, not a professional bookmaker character.
- His criminal organization is tied to narcotics trafficking and illegal gambling.
- The **Hotel Delphoria** is central to the original operation.
- The Delphoria gambling setup is accessed through/behind the hotel cocktail-lounge area in the original story world.
- Sonny Bonds infiltrates the gambling operation under an alias and plays poker with Bains and associates.
- Bains uses an alias in the gambling environment and initially presents himself with controlled confidence rather than constant shouting or theatrical menace.
- PQ2 preserves the Delphoria case as part of Bains' official history and greatly reinforces his violent reputation and the fear surrounding him.
- PQ3 continues the Bains-family shadow over Lytton but should be treated as legacy material, not direct Jesse characterization.

## Vocabulary buckets to mine

When building or revising Jesse's Hotline, search the Tier A material for these categories rather than copying prose wholesale:

- hotel front desk, lounge, penthouse, suite, lobby, room number, messages, telephone, elevator, service area;
- poker, cards, ante, cash, money, gambling, game, player, buy-in-like context;
- runner, associate, contact, caller, front man, partner, witness, informant;
- street narcotics / criminal-network vocabulary appropriate to late-1980s Sierra writing;
- police case-file terms used to describe Bains from the outside;
- short direct Bains dialogue showing cadence, patience, impatience, confidence and threat level.

## Copyright / production rule

Do not commit full manuals, transcript dumps, hint books or scans from third-party archives into this repository unless the user supplies a lawful copy specifically for project use. Keep this repository to:

- source URLs and provenance;
- concise factual notes;
- short, necessary excerpts;
- derived character/style rules written in our own language.

## Operational boundary

This reference index informs character voice and fictional presentation only. It cannot change Betting Edge recommendations, prices, statuses, stakes, risk, model outputs or evidence standards.
