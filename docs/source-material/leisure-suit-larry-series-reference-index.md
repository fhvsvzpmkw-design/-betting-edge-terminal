## High-value character principles to mine

Across the classic material, extract patterns rather than copying dialogue wholesale:

- Larry believes he is smoother, more desirable and more socially competent than the world around him suggests.
- The joke frequently lands on Larry himself: failed assumptions, humiliation, overconfidence, bad timing and pathetic recovery.
- Larry can be crude, but comic self-delusion is more important than explicit sexual language.
- Narrator/scene commentary is a major part of classic Larry humor; the world often knows Larry is ridiculous before Larry does.
- Cheap hotels, casinos, lounges, resorts, cruise ships, bars, cabs, tourist traps, tacky signage and bargain-basement sophistication are natural Larry environments.
- Larry's optimism is unusually durable. Rejection becomes evidence, in his mind, that the next attempt will go better.
- Larry should sound like a character from the classic Sierra comedy world, not like a modern pickup influencer or explicit pornographic narrator.

## Copyright / production rule

Do not commit full manuals, game scripts, walkthrough dumps, screenshots, design documents or scans from external archives into this repository unless the user supplies a lawful copy specifically for project use.

Keep this repository to:

- source URLs and provenance;
- concise factual notes;
- short, necessary excerpts when genuinely useful;
- derived character/style rules written in our own language.
