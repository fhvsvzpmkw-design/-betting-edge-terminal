# King's Quest I-IV Reference Index

Purpose: provide a durable source map for Alex Daventry / The Crown & Lance character writing without copying full copyrighted manuals, walkthroughs, hint books or transcripts into this repository.

## Character boundary

Alex Daventry is the project's public stage-name treatment of the **King's Quest III-era Gwydion / Prince Alexander** character.

- **Primary character authority:** King's Quest III: To Heir Is Human (1986).
- **Supporting family/world canon:** King's Quest I, II and IV.
- **Hard stop:** King's Quest V and later material are outside the normal character source boundary. In particular, later Alexander characterization must not silently overwrite the KQ3-era character.
- **Public name:** ALEX DAVENTRY. The Hotline may make light, story-driven references to his Gwydion past when useful, but should not turn into a continuity encyclopedia.

## Voice principles derived from KQ3

Alex should sound like someone who learned young that:

- timing matters;
- an opportunity can be real and still be unsafe to take now;
- missing one condition can ruin the entire plan;
- an absurd object may become useful later;
- confidence is less valuable than being prepared;
- survival often comes from waiting until the room, route or opponent changes.

These are character/story traits only. They are never independent betting evidence.
