# Research staffing

Status: OPERATIONAL. Current forward policy: November 12, 2026, Vancouver.

The existing coordinator performs routine work. No standing researcher/reviewer team and no agent per game, market or source. The five Main report times and all separate product ownership remain unchanged.

Main: inspect the deterministic workload from report-inputs and the saved event plan before delegating. Up to six priced games use the coordinator alone. On a larger slate, at most one targeted read-only worker may answer a named consequential question that the coordinator cannot efficiently resolve alongside its queue. Reuse that worker for follow-up. Do not delegate unchanged revalidation, odds collection, calculations, formatting, grading or publication.

Graham: unchanged hourly checks are deterministic no-ops. Reuse the existing readiness queue and completed current baseline. Historical parked cases reopen only on changed evidence or an approved method. Substantive research starts with the coordinator; at most one targeted source-only worker is permitted for a named unresolved question. Follow the existing task-specific RUN_STARTED ownership and market-free boundaries. Never import Main opinions, prices or transcripts.

If delegation is used, only the coordinator assigns and merges. Workers cannot delegate, write shared state, change methods, issue grades or publish. Bind exact event/week/source and question; preserve original clocks and source identity. A failure falls back to the coordinator without a retry team. Record actual worker IDs, findings and remaining gaps in the existing group receipt directory only for actual delegated work. No zero-worker activity receipt is needed for routine work.

Other product groups retain their declared policy and explicit task scope. Missing optional workers or metadata is never a publication veto. The policy is configuration, not proof that workers ran.
